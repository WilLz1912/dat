imagine how bad this is going to be with later monsters that will chain aoe knockdown attacks together, with high damage. In Wilds some improvements have been
made to the combat. Some weapons are quicker in use. There is a focus mode, so your attacks swing where the camera is targeted, rather than having to move in

tools. In world using your potions and sharpening is way more dangerous. Going from Wilds to this makes this system even more egregious. I hate the routines I
