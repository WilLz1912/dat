Absolutely brilliant game, but you will need mods to realize its full potential. Specifically, Content manager, Pure, a post-processing filter of your choice,
and then car/track mods on top. Once you get that, you will have the virtual embodiment of a mechanic's wet dream. You get an incredible handling and physics
model, a very expandable visual engine, and an OK soundtrack in the way of engine sounds (currently the most lacking aspect, even after modding). But to me, all
of those still pale in comparison to the mechanical sandbox you get to have in front of you with this game. Instead of naming every option you have at your
disposal, let me just describe some cars that I built in-game, all just by editing existing mods, or rather their files. 1. The world's strangest presidential
vehicle - While perusing the internet one day, I came across the Audi A8 L Security - a 4 ton armored beast meant for VIPs. Now, I personally don't like that
gen of the A8, so instead I decided to edit a different car in this fashion. Naturally, it was a Mini Cooper Countryman, because why not. I edited it to weigh
3,9 tons and push 250 horsepower, and adjusted the suspension until it actually carried that weight quite graciously. 2. A safe Dodge Viper - this being a Viper
that was converted to be FWD, have ABS and TC, and one which got its V10 replaced with the Fiat 600D's 32 horsepower inline 4. Perfect grocery getter. 3. The
most annoying luxury vehicle - specifically, a 2020 Maybach S650 with the engine out of a third-gen Honda Civic. The 7-speed automatic stayed, with its gear
ratios adjusted to achieve a fairly impressive 210 km/h top speed. 4. A WRC-inspired Citroen C4 VTS build - which was a more serious, detailed build than the
others. Power went from 150 to 215 hp, the suspension went up by 7 cm and was made softer, and the whole thing was tuned for rugged mountain roads and light
offroading. Was one of the more fun builds. I made these and so many more, and still plan on some more goofy builds like a diesel-powered RX7 because again, why
not. Point being, you can adjust just about everything on cars. If you are in any way mechanically inclined, this is THE game to play. Plus, it regularly hits 5
euros which is a steal for what it offers. Get the DLC as well.
